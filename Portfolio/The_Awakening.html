I felt a trapped, ancient heat bleeding into my skin.
I felt a calling that no one else could hear. The Purest Sacrifice the world shattered and my sun and stars fell, my grief did not paralyse me; it clarified me. To wake the dragon, a price must be paid. Blood for blood. Life for life.

The Roaring Crucible

As the torch met the oil, the flames erupted, a towering wall of shifting crimson, gold, and predatory violet. The heat forced the khalasar back, but it pulled me forward.
I stepped away from the dirt of exile.

The fire consumed my clothes, my hair, and the fragile remnants of the girl I used to be. It peeled away the layers of the beggar queen until only the raw, indestructible truth remained.
