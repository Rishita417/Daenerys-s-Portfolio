The breaker of Chains They wanted me to rule the old world. They wanted me to play by their cruel, centuries-old rules. But I did not cross the sea to sit on top of a rotten system.

The City of Chains

I marched upon Slaver’s Bay and found a world built entirely on human agony. In Astapor, Yunkai, and Meereen, human lives were traded like livestock, and the wealthy sat in high pyramids built on the backs of the bleeding. The masters told me this was the natural order. They told me the wheel of power had always turned this way: one house on top, then another, crushing the people on the bottom.

The Blood of the Unsullied

They thought they could buy me, or intimidate me with their armies. In Astapor, they traded me an army of eight thousand Unsullied for a single dragon. It was their fatal mistake.
I gave the command in High Valyrian: Dracarys.
I untied the whips from the masters' hands.
I handed the swords to the freed.
I did not buy an army; I forged a brotherhood of free men. They chose to follow me, not because of a master's whip, but because I looked at them and saw human beings.

The New Tide

The masters called me a conqueror, a tyrant, a savior, and a thief. The people called me Mhysa—Mother.
I nailed the masters to the posts where they had crucified children.
I broke open the gates of the fighting pits.
I tore down the golden harpies that crowned their cities.

Breaking the Wheel

They told me to choose a side in their ancient games. They asked how I would fit into the shifting political alliances of the great houses. House Lannister, House Baratheon, House Stark, House Tyrell—they are all just spokes on a wheel. It turns, and turns, crushing the people on the ground.
I am not here to keep the wheel turning. I am not here to change the spoke. I am here to break the wheel.
